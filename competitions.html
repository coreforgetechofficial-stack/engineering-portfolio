What is FIRST?

FIRST stands for For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology. It was founded by inventor Dean Kamen in 1989 to help young people fall in love with engineering. Teams build real robots, play a brand-new game every season, and — the best part for me — the FIRST Championship has been held in Houston since 2017. 🏆

FRC vs FTC

Two competitions, two sizes of robot — same amount of brainpower.

High school

🏆 FRC — FIRST Robotics Competition

  • 🤖 Big robots — up to ~125 lb
  • ⏱️ A brand-new game every January + a 6-week build season
  • 🆚 3v3 matches on a giant field
  • 🧠 Teams design, build, wire, and program everything themselves
Middle + high school · ages 12–18

🎯 FTC — FIRST Tech Challenge

  • 📦 Robot starts inside an 18-inch cube
  • ⚔️ 2v2 matches on a 12 ft × 12 ft field
  • 📱 Runs on an Android-powered Control Hub
  • 🌱 A perfect stepping stone into FRC later
